# Great Waters Gazette

A compact morning PDF digest for Gary.

## Editorial rules

- Publish one consequential, genuinely new story per approved topic, except Lighting Industry, which carries two.
- Lead the news with one AI Story of the Day selected from the most consequential credible development in the preceding 24–48 hours. Rank consequence above raw virality; use social conversation, creator attention and Nathaniel Whittemore's AI Daily Brief as discovery and importance signals, then verify the story with a primary source or established reporting.
- Before selecting stories, check recent Gazette archives and `story-history.md` for previously used headlines, developments, and URLs.
- Never repeat a previously published story or repackage the same underlying development with a different headline.
- Lighting Industry carries the two most important genuinely current developments published within the preceding seven calendar days. This is a hard cutoff: verify the source's publication date, never widen the window, and do not treat a future impact date as making an older article current. Undated items do not qualify. If fewer than two worthwhile, non-repeated stories qualify, state that no second qualifying story was published during the seven-day window instead of using stale filler.
- Lake Oconee News requires a new story in every edition. If the usual sources have no update, broaden the source set and reasonable lookback window until a distinct, relevant story is found.
- After publishing, add every selected headline, source URL, and edition date to `story-history.md`.
- Prefer primary sources, established reporting, and local outlets for local news.
- Use one concise sentence per story with a clickable source link. The complete summary and its link must fit on one rendered line; shorten the summary rather than wrapping or over-compressing the type.
- When a section contains two stories, render each story on its own separate line.
- Favor consequential current news over minor announcements, but never satisfy a section with a stale item or by repeating an earlier Gazette story.
- Microsoft 365 Copilot covers the paid, full Microsoft 365 work product. Pair the strongest current product update with one immediately useful premium-feature tip.
- ChatGPT pairs the strongest current product update with one immediately useful tip or workflow.
- Porsche 997 & 911 prioritizes standard road-car 997.1 and 997.2 coverage: Carrera, Carrera S, Carrera 4/4S, Targa, Turbo and Cabriolet ownership, maintenance, reliability, buying guides, technical guidance, tasteful modifications, restoration, market analysis and high-quality videos. Every selected link must be fully readable or watchable without payment, subscription, registration or login; reject paywalled, metered, preview-only and subscriber-only sources. Maintain a rolling queue of worthwhile non-repeated items published within the preceding three months and select the best unused item each day; same-day publication is not required. Do not use GT3, GT2, RS, race-car or modern-911 stories merely to fill the section. If no worthwhile unused standard 997.1/997.2 item remains, say so rather than substituting another GT-car story; broaden only to unusually relevant non-GT 911 road-car coverage.
- AI-Powered Solopreneur Business favors specific businesses a single operator can build with heavy AI leverage. Prefer concrete buyers, offers, revenue models, distribution paths, evidence and small validation tests; reject generic make-money-with-AI content.
- Keep a close runner-up only while it remains timely.
- Weather covers ZIP 31024 and links to The Weather Channel.
- Weather verification is a mandatory pre-publication gate. Open the live Weather Channel 10-day forecast and confirm that the page identifies the location as Eatonton, Georgia for ZIP 31024.
- Record the forecast page's visible "as of" time, then compare each of the five Gazette entries against the live page: calendar date, condition, precipitation wording when relevant, high, and low.
- Do not infer, estimate, reuse, or carry forward weather values from an earlier edition. If the Weather Channel page cannot be loaded and verified, stop publication and report that the weather source could not be confirmed.
- After generating the PDF, extract its five weather rows and compare them a second time with the verified values before delivery.
- The daily photograph must have clear reuse rights, a caption, and attribution.

## Design

- US Letter, always one page.
- Cream background, black serif typography, dark navy dividers.
- Compact weather at upper left; a larger selected photograph at upper right.
- Place the Stoic practice directly below the weather/photo block and above every news section.
- Leave a slightly larger visual gap after the Stoic practice before the first news section.
- Rebalance section spacing to use the page naturally: avoid both crowded sections and a large unused block at the bottom.
- Stable output naming: `Great_Waters_Gazette_YYYY-MM-DD.pdf`.
