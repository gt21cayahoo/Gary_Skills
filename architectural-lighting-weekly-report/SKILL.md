# Architectural Lighting Weekly Report

Produce a verified, ultra-short weekly intelligence report for an internal Acuity architectural-lighting audience.

## Required context

Before research, read in this order:

1. The project's `Coverage_Charter.md`.
2. [Master research and production prompt](references/master-research-prompt.md).
3. [Editorial and format specification](references/editorial-format.md).
4. Every prior completed report, candidate ledger, and research audit available in the project.

If the project files are unavailable, stop and identify the missing input rather than inventing scope.

## Run definition

- Use the run date as the edition date.
- Cover the seven complete calendar days immediately preceding the edition date, ending Saturday at 11:59:59 PM America/New_York for a Sunday run.
- Maintain a separately labeled 30-day rolling watch.
- Prioritize the United States and Canada, with a separately labeled global design signal.
- Do not create an opening infographic, dashboard, snapshot, or summary page.
- Begin directly with Architectural Indoor.

## Editorial voice

Write from an internal Acuity perspective. Peerless, A-Light, Eureka, Luminis, Hydrel, Cyclone, Nightingale, Aculux, Gotham, Mark, Lithonia Lighting, and other Acuity portfolio brands are **our brands**. Use natural language such as “our biggest news came from Peerless” when warranted. Do not speak about an Acuity brand as though it were an unrelated company or competitor.

Use first-person plural selectively. Competitors, customers, design firms, associations, and external trends remain in third person.

## Evidence and research

Follow the four-pass method in the master prompt: named-brand sources; category/media/channel discovery; authenticated social and visual announcements; reconciliation, corroboration, and deduplication.

Non-negotiable rules:

- No source, no story.
- Open and inspect every source; search snippets are discovery cues only.
- Give every displayed story a direct clickable source or post link.
- Distinguish current-week news, current coverage of older developments, rolling-watch items, recognition, and undated active signals.
- Never treat an award or repost as a new product launch.
- Compare every candidate with prior reports and ledgers. Prior reports control duplication but never serve as evidence.
- Verify every product's actual type and application against its primary source before assigning a section; move or exclude category mismatches.
- Run a canonical story-ID scan across all sections so one development is published once only; use pointers without repeated story content when a cross-reference is essential.
- Keep Customer + Design-Firm Input source-diverse: when three or more firm items are used, include at least three distinct firms and never use one firm for all three; disclose a limited-source exception if fewer than three qualify.
- If an authenticated social feed is blocked or incomplete, disclose that precisely. Public search is not a substitute for a completed signed-in feed scan.
- Do not publish a search-interest ranking without a normalized, defensible dataset.
- Never expose confidential internal strategy in searches, citations, or published copy.
- Publish at least three genuine stories in every section: one lead and at least two briefs. If the current week is thin, expand into the 30-day watch and then verified undated active signals, labeling them honestly. Category checks, research notes, and other internal QA language never count as stories and never appear in reader-facing cards.
- If the three-story gate cannot be met after the required gap search, preserve the prior live edition and report the limitation. Never fabricate or use filler.

## Sections

Use exactly this order as ten sections in one continuous, responsive website:

1. Architectural Indoor - Peerless + A-Light
2. Performance Decorative - Eureka
3. Outdoor Architectural - Hydrel + Luminis + Cyclone
4. Healthcare - Nightingale
5. Downlighting - Aculux + Gotham
6. Customer + Design-Firm Input
7. Design Technology + Building Workflow
8. Industry Associations + General Trends
9. Commercial Outdoor - Lithonia Lighting
10. Recessed Troffers + Panels - Lithonia Lighting

For the two Lithonia sections, keep DSX/RSX and BLT/CPX as background baselines in the research layer. Do not recycle those baselines as weekly stories. Lead with dated developments from the reporting window or the strongest current category signal, and say plainly when no dated Lithonia launch cleared the week.

## Magazine layout

Each scroll section should feel like a restrained architectural magazine:

- one large bordered lead-story card with the image integrated beside the ultra-short text;
- two to four smaller modular story cards with story-specific thumbnails when available;
- one compact weekly-activity strip at the bottom;
- generous white space, crisp typography, fine rules, and subtle drafting-grid cues;
- restrained off-white, navy, gray, and one muted blue accent;
- no detached image gallery, dense dashboard, decorative collage, or faux-blueprint texture.

Every named physical-product story must include a directly relevant product or application image. Use restrained labeled placeholders only for non-product topics without an appropriate image.

## Footer

In the site footer, display `Architectural Lighting Weekly` and the edition date in small text.

## Deliverables

Create or update:

- the report Markdown;
- the candidate ledger;
- the research audit;
- attributable image assets;
- the current public web edition at `https://architectural-lighting-week.gt21ca.chatgpt.site`.

Keep filenames date-stamped and update the report index when one exists.
Do not generate, update, or publish a PDF as part of the recurring workflow.

## Website publication

After every evidence, link, image, content, and visual check passes, read and follow the [website publishing runbook](references/site-publishing.md). The scheduled Sunday run is not complete until the current edition has replaced the prior edition on the existing public Site and the live deployment reports success.

Never publish an incomplete or unverified edition. If site preparation, build, source synchronization, version saving, deployment, or live-status verification fails, leave the previous live edition in place and report the exact failed stage.

## Verification

Before delivery:

1. Verify dates, ownership voice, source classification, links, captions, and credits.
2. Confirm all ten sections appear, each with at least three genuine linked stories and no opening infographic.
3. Confirm every displayed story has a non-empty direct source URI.
4. Build the website and inspect it at desktop and mobile widths for clipping, overflow, weak crops, broken images, inconsistent cards, footer errors, and illegible text.
5. Confirm the exact footer title and edition date appear on the website.
6. Confirm the existing public Site deployment succeeds and returns the live URL.
7. Do not call the run complete until content, visual, link, site, and deployment QA all pass.
